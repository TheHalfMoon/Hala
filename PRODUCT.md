# Hala product intent

Build a compact commercially usable multimodal intelligence service for Aladdin Pro. The product must understand English and Arabic, plan bounded tasks, ground GUI targets, propose allowed actions, and route cheaply. Local Aladdin policy and independent verification remain authoritative.

First deliver independently testable contracts, synthetic environments, a mock inference service, and source-grounded model and data research. Real model qualification, tuning, quantization, and cost decisions require actual remote runtime evidence. No model deployment or paid infrastructure is authorized.
