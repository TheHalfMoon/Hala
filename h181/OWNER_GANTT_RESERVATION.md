## Boundary to actual model progress

The owner check covers only *surviving local Git* checkouts. It cannot
prove whether other historic repositories were forked, deleted, archived,
copied, installed as npm packages, or used on another machine. Therefore
even a PASS **does not prove true OOD novelty**. Treat this as only a
tentative source reservation; a later complete independent historical
source audit must approve it before any one-shot OOD capture.

TRAIN/DEV admission is separately blocked until independent prior
PNG/RGBA SHA-only history and source-fork provenance checks are complete.
Any future TRAIN/DEV use of Pickr or interact.js must gather new genuine
native data, keep original MIT notices intact, include bilingual tasks,
validate independent exact decoded-RGBA replay, and keep the Gantt reserved
source entirely out of train, tuning, DEV and prompt engineering.

Only after model and evaluation protocol are frozen should the reserved
Gantt candidate be captured once and truly untouched OOD be scored.
The consumed H173 v3 result (2/32 vs 3/32, p=1.0) cannot be reused
for model selection or treated as an improvement.

**Do not merge, deploy, publish, train, generate new OOD images, or
claim any model improvement from H181.** Keep the work Draft.
